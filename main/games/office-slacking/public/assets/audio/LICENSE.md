# Office sound credits

These recordings are bundled locally. Short excerpts were downmixed and encoded to OGG Vorbis for the game. Original archives are not needed at runtime.

| Files | Author and source | License / changes |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `drink.ogg`, `cloth.ogg` | OwlishMedia — [202 More Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/202-more-sound-effects) | CC0 1.0; Drink_01 (+10 dB) / Cloth_01, mono 24 kHz |

The air-conditioning bed, soft object contact and breath fallback are generated with Web Audio. Playback applies volume, speed, distance filtering and stereo panning. The chatter is background atmosphere; character dialogue is displayed as text and has no recorded voice actor.
